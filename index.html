Questions

Things people reasonably ask.

Straight answers about what this work is, what it isn't, and how to begin. If your question isn't here, just ask.

What is entity SEO, in plain terms?

Entity SEO is the work of making sure search engines understand who you are as a distinct, real thing — not just a string of keywords. It connects your profiles, mentions, content and structured data so that when someone searches your name, the result describes one clear, accurate person or brand instead of a confusing mix of pages.

Why does my online identity matter at all?

People search before they trust. Clients, employers, collaborators and partners will look you up — and the internet will answer with whatever it finds. Entity work makes sure that answer is accurate, consistent and the one you'd choose, rather than something outdated, incomplete or contradictory.

Can you guarantee I'll get a Google Knowledge Panel?

No — and you should be cautious of anyone who says they can. Google alone decides whether a Knowledge panel is created, shown or updated. What can be done is preparation: strengthening the consistent, credible information footprint that makes a name eligible. That's the honest scope of this work.

What's the difference between a readiness check and full strategy work?

A readiness check is a focused assessment: how prepared your current footprint is, what's consistent, and what needs attention. Full strategy work goes further and actively shapes the entity over time — structured data, identity management and reputation consistency. The check is a sensible first step if you're unsure where you stand.

What do you need from me to get started?

A clear picture of who you are and how you want to be understood — your name as it should appear, the field you work in, and the profiles or sites that already represent you. From there we can identify inconsistencies and agree what to strengthen first. A short conversation is usually enough to know whether it's a good fit.

How do I know if my information is inconsistent online?

A few signs: your job title differs across profiles, an old role still shows up prominently, the same facts appear with different spellings or dates, or results for your name describe more than one version of you. If that sounds familiar, an audit is a practical way to get a clear, honest view of it.

Is this only for large companies or public figures?

No. Anyone whose name or brand is searched can benefit — professionals, founders, executives, creators and organisations of many sizes. The principle is the same whether you're a well-known figure or a specialist who simply wants to be found accurately.
