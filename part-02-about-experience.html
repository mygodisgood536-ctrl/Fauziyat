Building the identity behind the name.

My work sits at the intersection of entity SEO, structured data and digital reputation. In practice, that means helping search engines understand what an entity actually is — and making sure the information a person, founder or company puts out there tells one clear, consistent story.

The starting point is usually a question: when someone searches for this person or this company, what does the engine actually find? Often the answer is fragments — a name on one site, a different spelling on another, a profile with no context, an outdated mention that outranks the official page.

The approach is entity-first: research, website optimization, profile development, information alignment, schema recommendations and authority building. The aim is clarity, consistency and long-term discoverability — the kind of foundation that keeps working after any single campaign ends.
