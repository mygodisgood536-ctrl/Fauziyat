About

A specialist in how names are understood online.

I'm Fauziyat Folashade, a Search Engine Marketing Specialist based in Nigeria. My work centres on a question that matters more every year: when someone searches for a person or a brand, does the internet give back a clear, accurate and trustworthy answer?

Over the course of my career I've specialised in entity SEO — the discipline of making a name, and everything connected to it, unambiguous to search engines. That means shaping digital identity, keeping information consistent across the web, structuring data so it can be properly read, and protecting how a professional reputation is represented online.

My background in search engine marketing gives that work a practical, performance-minded edge: visibility that isn't just visible, but understood. I work with professionals, personal brands, founders, executives, companies and organisations who need their online presence to reflect who they actually are.

“A name online should mean one clear thing — not a dozen confusing ones.”
